## DESIGN FLAW FOUND & FIXED: policy ownership

First implementation registered policy objects from loader context →
root_owner = 0 → user 1000 could NEVER legitimately destroy inside its
own policy-protected trees ("DENIED cross-uid: root owned by 0").
Protection must not confiscate the owner's authority.

Fix (3 layers):
1. INODE_SET args[3] packs (owner<<32)|payload; high word 0 keeps
   legacy pm-mark semantics (owner = registering uid).
2. Policy grammar: `TREE <path> [U <uid>]`.
3. Issuer unchanged — it already stamps the PEER uid into FD caps.

Result: user-owned tree + FD capability chain works end-to-end:
request → SCM_RIGHTS fd → attach → exec rm → destroyed.
Root-owned lines (no U) remain root-only-delegable by design.
